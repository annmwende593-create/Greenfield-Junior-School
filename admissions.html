Admissions

Greenfield Junior School welcomes applications from parents and guardians seeking quality education for their children.

Admission Requirements

  • A completed application form.
  • A copy of the learner's birth certificate.
  • Previous school reports, where applicable.
  • Passport-size photographs of the learner.
  • Parent or guardian contact information.

Application Procedure

  1. Visit the school or contact the administration.
  2. Obtain and complete the admission application form.
  3. Submit the required documents.
  4. Attend an assessment or interview if required.
  5. Receive admission confirmation from the school.
